My ETFs: Look-Through Valuation

Each ETF is valued through its published top holdings (funds of funds such as XEQT are expanded into their underlying ETFs). Every valued holding contributes its weight × (DCF value / price), giving the intrinsic value of $1 invested in the valued part of the fund. Banks and insurers are not valued, and a DCF value below zero counts as zero.
